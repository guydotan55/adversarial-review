# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-29

Initial public release.

- The `adversarial-review` skill: independent fresh reviewer, severity ranking (Blocker, Major, Minor), binary verdict, review-revise-re-review loop.
- Seeds (already-verified facts) with a required "Seeds checked" section.
- Panel escalation with per-launch consent, and a panel playbook in `references/`.
- Evals: six scenarios, a planted-flaw spec with answer key, and logged results.
